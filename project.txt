1. Project Overview
Billza is a Stellar-integrated bill management platform designed to help individuals, households, freelancers, and small businesses organize recurring financial obligations, receive payment reminders, and initiate supported payments from one dashboard.
Users can create bill records for electricity, internet, rent, subscriptions, and other utilities, assign due dates and recurring schedules, and track whether bills are upcoming, overdue, or paid.
Billza will also integrate Stellar wallet functionality, enabling users to initiate supported Testnet payments and associate confirmed blockchain transactions with their bill records.
The platform focuses on five capabilities:
Bill Management Dashboard: Create, edit, organize, and monitor bills.
Automated Reminder System: Receive alerts for upcoming and overdue bills.
Stellar Payment Flow: Initiate and verify payments using supported Stellar assets.
Bill Search & Filtering: Quickly locate bills by category, date, and status.
Recurring Bill Management: Automatically generate or advance bill schedules according to configured frequencies.
The goal is to make recurring financial obligations easier to manage while demonstrating how Stellar can support everyday payment workflows.
The first MVP will use Stellar Testnet. It will not claim to settle real utility-provider accounts without an authorized provider integration.
2. Problem Being Solved
Individuals and businesses frequently manage bills through fragmented systems such as banking applications, spreadsheets, messages, and handwritten reminders.
This creates several challenges:
Forgotten payment deadlines.
Missed recurring subscriptions.
Difficulty tracking monthly expenses.
Poor visibility into paid and unpaid bills.
Inconsistent payment records.
Difficulty finding previous transactions.
Limited practical payment-management tools for Stellar users.
Billza addresses these challenges by combining recurring bill organization, reminders, and blockchain-based payment records in one application.

4. Core User Journeys
Bill Creation
User visits Billza.
Creates an account.
Opens the dashboard.
Selects Add New Bill.
Enters bill name, category, amount, and due date.
Selects payment frequency.
Adds recipient payment information, if applicable.
Saves the bill.
Bill appears on the dashboard.
Reminder Journey
User creates a bill.
Sets reminder preferences.
Billza calculates the next due date.
Reminder service identifies an upcoming bill.
User receives an email or in-app notification.
User opens the bill details.
User initiates payment or marks the bill as manually paid.
Stellar Payment Journey
User opens an unpaid bill.
Selects Pay with Stellar.
Connects a compatible Stellar wallet.
Reviews the payment amount, asset, and recipient.
Signs the transaction.
Stellar processes the payment.
Billza verifies the transaction.
Payment record is associated with the bill.
Bill status updates.
User can view the transaction on Stellar Explorer.
Recurring Bill Journey
User creates a recurring bill.
Selects weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly frequency.
Billza calculates the next payment date.
The current bill occurrence is tracked.
Once the occurrence is paid, the next occurrence is scheduled.
The reminder service continues monitoring future due dates.
Core Product Flow:
Create Bill → Configure Recurrence → Schedule Reminder → Receive Alert → Initiate Stellar Payment → Verify Transaction → Update Bill Status → Track Next Due Date

Actual utility-provider bill settlement requires a supported payment or billing integration and is outside this sprint.

Final Team Instruction: Billza must demonstrate a working bill management and Stellar payment application, not simply a dashboard displaying manually entered expenses.
The priority is a reliable end-to-end workflow:
Bill Creation → Recurring Schedule → Automated Reminder → Stellar Payment → On-Chain Verification → Payment Record → Next Due Date
The team should prioritize accurate bill scheduling, dependable reminders, and a fully verified Stellar Testnet payment flow within the 30-day development period.
